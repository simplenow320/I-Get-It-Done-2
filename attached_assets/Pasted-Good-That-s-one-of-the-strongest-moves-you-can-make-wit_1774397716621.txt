🧠 The Idea: “Dump Times”

You’re not just adding reminders.

You’re creating anchors in a person’s day.

👉 That’s what builds habit.

⸻

🎯 The 3 Dump Times (Keep It Simple)

Don’t overcomplicate this. Start with 2, optionally 3.

1. Morning Dump (High Priority)

Purpose: Clear the mind + set direction

Trigger time: 7am–9am (user adjustable later)

Prompt:
	•	“Start your day. Dump what’s on your mind.”
	•	“What needs your attention today?”

👉 This becomes their daily starting ritual

⸻

2. Midday Reset (Optional but Powerful)

Purpose: Regain focus

Trigger time: 12pm–2pm

Prompt:
	•	“Quick reset. What’s still on your plate?”
	•	“Refocus. What matters right now?”

👉 Helps people who fall off during the day

⸻

3. Evening Dump (High Priority)

Purpose: Closure + mental relief

Trigger time: 7pm–10pm

Prompt:
	•	“Wrap your day. What did you finish?”
	•	“Clear your mind before tomorrow.”

👉 This is huge for retention

⸻

🔥 Why This Works

You’re aligning with natural behavior patterns:
	•	Morning → planning
	•	Midday → correction
	•	Evening → reflection

👉 Now your app fits life instead of interrupting it

⸻

⚙️ What To Tell Your Dev (Simple Build)

Feature: “Daily Dump Times”

1. Default Setup (No friction)
	•	Morning dump ON by default
	•	Evening dump ON by default
	•	Midday optional toggle

⸻

2. Notification System
	•	Push notifications at selected times
	•	Simple text only, no overload

⸻

3. One-Tap Entry

When user clicks notification:
	•	Opens directly to voice input
	•	No extra screens

⸻

4. Dashboard Sync

After they open:
	•	Show “Start Your Dump” card if not completed
	•	After completion, replace with:
	•	“You’re done for now”
	•	Or next suggested action

⸻

⚠️ Keep This Tight

Do NOT:
	•	Add complex scheduling UI
	•	Add too many options
	•	Add customization early

👉 Start simple:
	•	Morning + Evening default
	•	Let usage guide expansion
